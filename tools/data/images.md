## Best image to check your drawing against (not downloaded, licence unclear)

- **Joseph H. Peterson's clean redrawing of the Sloane 3188 seal**, about 1600×1600, black and white:
  https://www.esotericarchives.com/dee/aemethd.jpg
  Every ring number, the 49 letters, the names of God with their small numbers, the pentagram names and the Levanael cross are readable. I used it to read the positions in `seal.json`. Licence: none stated. It is a modern redrawing from Peterson's edition of Dee (© Joseph H. Peterson), so treat it as **copyrighted, for reference only**. Do not bundle it in the app.

## Tips for checking your drawing

- Rotate any reference so that cell 1 (T with a 4 above it) sits just clockwise of 12 o'clock, and the outer heptagon's vertex points straight up.
- Lettering is radial with tops outward, so the bottom half of the seal reads upside-down. Most popular redrawings get this right.
